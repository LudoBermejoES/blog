## Who I Am

I'm Ludo. I work as a technology director, or CTO. I'm also a creative writing teacher, a writer, a tabletop RPG game master, a former LARP director and, now and then, I do a few things with graphics engines.

## What This Is About

This blog will be about technology, writing, teaching, roleplaying games, software, 3D, games — all mixed together. I like to crossbreed what I know at every level.
